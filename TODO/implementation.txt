- claude: guard training/test inputs against being multiples of each other (cosine check).
  Without bias terms the net is positively homogeneous, N(c*x) = c*N(x), measured
  as exactly 2.000 for N(2x)/N(x). So a test input that is a multiple of a training
  input is answered correctly for free and proves nothing - seen in practice: a
  scaled 3x input passed with error 1.2e-05 while a genuinely fresh input failed
  with 9.0e+01. Two proportional training inputs are also redundant, the second
  adds no equations. Reject candidates with |cosine| > 0.99 against the ones
  already accepted. Only matters for hand picked or systematic inputs; random
  vectors are almost never near parallel.
